## Portfolio integrity

The public residential and commercial imagery did not expose dependable project titles, locations, years, clients or detailed case-study metadata. The redesign therefore groups that work into neutral verified collections. It does not add fictional names, locations, dates, clients, budgets, areas, awards or credentials.

Journal titles and dates come from the published site. The original article pages exposed little or no body content, so the concise article copy in this repository is limited to the same verified services and six-stage process rather than introducing new business claims.

No verified public social-profile links were found, so none are displayed.
